# Say the language when you call

Canby Community Clinic sees people in English and in Spanish. The office is at 7601 Canby Ave #6B in Reseda, weekdays from 9 AM to 5 PM. The useful moment to say which language you want is on the phone, (818) 674-4414, or at the desk when you arrive. Not halfway through an explanation of a medicine.

If you need an interpreter in another language, ask then too. If you are calling for a parent, say which language they want. The clinic will tell you what can be arranged that day.

## When a relative usually translates

Families do this because it works, until it does not. A child should not be the person explaining a new diagnosis, a dose, or a result that scared you. A spouse may already be carrying the whole visit. You can ask for the explanation again in the language you think in, and you can ask a relative to wait outside for part of it.

Bring the words you do not want translated loosely. The name of the medicine. The allergy and what happened when you took it. The symptom you have been minimizing. Write them in whichever language is clearer. The list can be five lines.

Before you leave, repeat the plan in your own words: what you will take, what you will watch for, and when you come back or call. If the version you just said does not match what you heard, that is the time to fix it. Nodding through a plan you cannot follow is how doses get missed.
