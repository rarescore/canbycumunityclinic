# 7601 Canby Ave #6B

Canby Community Clinic is in Reseda, in suite 6B. The clinic used to be called Pura Vida Community Clinic. The address did not change. Weekdays, 9 AM to 5 PM. The phone is (818) 674-4414.

People come from the neighborhood and from Canoga Park, Winnetka, Northridge, and other parts of the west Valley. If you are coming from farther east in Los Angeles, call before you drive. Distance is a real part of whether a clinic works for you.

## What this office is for

A checkup. Follow-up of something already known and stable. Blood pressure. A conversation about diabetes risk or cholesterol. A question about a medicine you take. Help arranging a basic lab when a clinician orders one. The visit can be in English or Spanish.

The same week, other problems belong somewhere else. A sprain you need seen tonight, a fever that worries you before morning, a cut that may need stitches: that is urgent care. Chest pain, trouble breathing, face drooping, arm weakness, trouble speaking, severe bleeding, fainting, or confusion: call 911.

## Getting in

Walk in on a weekday during open hours, or leave your name and phone if you want a time held. A request on the website is a request for a call back. The time is set when someone at the clinic confirms it.

Bring a photo ID if you have one, the medicines you take, and an insurance card if you have one. If you do not have a card, you can still come. Say that when you call, or say it at the desk.
